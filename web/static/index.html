How it works

One agent. Two surfaces. Zero guesswork.

Built on Hermes Agent in three connected layers. HQ and the store floor talk to the same Kuya, the same playbook and the same data.

FACE

Hermes Desktop + Telegram

HQ clicks Run full sweep. Branch managers text Kuya in Taglish from the floor.

PLAYBOOK

kuya-hermes-ops skill

Turns a request into steps: detect → check → propose → ask for your yes → act.

HANDS

suki MCP server

9 domain tools: sweep, pulse, restock check, PO, shift cover. No "run any SQL".

DATA

Suki Mart store.db

12 branches, about 230k rows, six months of history. Every number comes from here.

What Kuya does

From "may problema" to "naayos na" in one chat.

Network sweep

Ranks all 12 branches by risk in under a second: stockouts with no PO, duplicate POs, short shifts, unanswered tickets, late deliveries. network_sweep

Branch pulse

One look at a branch's stock, staffing for the next 2 days, tickets and deliveries. Branches without delivery are never told to fix deliveries. branch_pulse

Duplicate-PO guard

Checks for an open purchase order before reordering, and judges urgency by the supplier's real lead time, not its promise. check_restock create_purchase_order

Shift cover in one message

"Di pumasok si John bukas." Kuya finds the shift and proposes the most reliable free colleague, then books it after you say yes. find_shift_cover assign_cover

On the store floor

Text it like you'd text your kuya.

Branch managers are busy. They don't open dashboards; they send a quick Taglish message and get back a fix to approve.

  • Understands Taglish: ubos, pa-order, di pumasok, bukas
  • Only final answers on Telegram, with no tool noise
  • Nothing changes in the system until you say oo
