Architecture & Safety

One copilot. Two surfaces. Zero guesswork.

Kuya Hermes runs as an integrated operational loop. Floor staff and HQ leadership share identical data, safety invariants, and execution tools.

Surface Layer

Hermes Desktop & Telegram

HQ runs sweeps and multi-branch audits. Floor managers send concise Taglish audio and text updates directly from the aisles.

Playbook Layer

kuya-hermes-ops Skill

Structured operational workflows: detect anomalies, cross-reference inventory, propose remedies, and require human sign-off.

Tools Layer

suki MCP Server

9 typed domain tools. Validates duplicate PO guards and supplier lead time formulas. Arbitrary SQL queries are prohibited.

Data Layer

Suki Mart store.db

High-integrity SQLite store tracking 12 branches, 6 months of historical shift attendance, supplier deliveries, and ticket queues.

Core Capabilities

From alert to resolution in one message.

Network Risk Sweep

Ranks all 12 branches by risk in under a second: unreplenished stockouts, duplicate purchase orders, short shifts, and overdue customer tickets. network_sweep

Branch Pulse Telemetry

Unified diagnostic view of branch inventory, next-48-hour staffing schedule, and delivery reliability. Walk-in-only branches skip delivery checks automatically. branch_pulse

Duplicate-PO Protection

Prevents redundant purchase orders and judges restock urgency using empirical historical supplier delivery times rather than vendor promises. check_restock

Absence Coverage Matching

When an employee calls in sick, Kuya finds the open shift and identifies the most reliable eligible colleague based on attendance history. find_shift_cover
